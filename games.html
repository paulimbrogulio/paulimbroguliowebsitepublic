Adding a game

Copy one whole <div class="game-card"> block below and paste it inside the grid. The grid reflows on its own — you don't need to change any layout to fit a fourth or a tenth.

Card 1 — three things to change

1. The grey tag line, 2. the title, 3. the description.

4. And the one that actually matters: swap REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_FOLDER_ID in the link for your real Google Drive folder ID, then set that folder's sharing to Anyone with the link. A card with real text and a dead link is the easy mistake here.
